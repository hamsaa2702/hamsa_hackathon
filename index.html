How ResolveTrack works

A single workflow for local issues, from submission to resolution.

🔄 Complaint workflow

1. Submit a complaint
Citizen adds a description, category, location, and optional evidence.
2. Route to a department
Category-based rules suggest the department responsible for the issue.
3. Assign and investigate
An administrator assigns staff, who add notes and status updates.
4. Resolve or escalate
The issue is marked resolved or escalated if further attention is needed.

🛡️ Demo limitations

This is a front-end prototype. Records and uploaded evidence are stored locally in this browser. Role switching is for demonstration only and does not enforce real security. No live department contacts, maps, emails, or AI services are connected.

For production, connect a secure backend, database, authenticated user accounts, private file storage, and verified department contact data.
